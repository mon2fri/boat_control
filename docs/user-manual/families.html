Make reusable lists

A family gives one name to several choices you often pick together.

Make a list of columns

  1. Open Column/Value Family and click Add family.
  2. For Kind, choose Column Family.
  3. Give the list a name, such as Account details. Under Columns, select the items to put in it, such as Owner and Region. Click Create.
  4. Use the family wherever the column picker offers it. On the upload screen, look for Add column family. Check which columns were added, especially when your new files have different headings.

Make a list of entries used in filters or checks

  1. Click Add family and choose Value Family.
  2. Name it, for example Singapore and Malaysia.
  3. Under Owner columns, choose the column where those entries belong, such as Region.
  4. Under Values, add each entry as it appears in the file. Click Create.
  5. In a filter or rule, choose Region first, then choose the family from the available entries.

Use Edit to change a list, then click Update. Before clicking Remove, check whether a saved setup or rule still uses it.
